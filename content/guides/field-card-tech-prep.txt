FREE OFFLINE MAPS (already on your phone)

❑ Google Maps: Search area > menu > Download offline map. Expires in 30 days.

❑ Apple Maps (iOS 17+): Profile > Offline Maps > Download New Map.

❑ Organic Maps: Open app, zoom to area, auto-downloads. Free forever.

BATTERY + POWER

❑ Low Power Mode: Enable at trailhead. iPhone: Settings > Battery. Android: Battery Saver.

❑ Screen brightness: Reduce to 30-40%. #1 battery drain on any phone.

❑ Close apps: Swipe away social media and unused background apps.

❑ Airplane mode: ONLY if you have a separate satellite communicator. Otherwise leave cellular ON.

❑ Power bank: Day: 5K mAh (1 charge). Overnight: 10K. Multi-day: 20K mAh.

❑ Cold weather: Keep phone + bank in inside pockets near body. Cold cuts capacity 20-40%.

SATELLITE EMERGENCY + COVERAGE

❑ Satellite 911 texting: FREE for all carriers via T-Mobile/Starlink (emergency 911 only). Full messaging: $10/mo.

❑ iPhone 14+ SOS: Built-in satellite SOS, no subscription. Practice in Settings > Emergency SOS.

❑ Garmin inReach: Two-way satellite texts + SOS with 24/7 monitoring. ~$15/mo. Best for backcountry.
