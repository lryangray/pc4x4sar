BEFORE YOU LEAVE

❑ Weather: Check forecast for route + destination. Pack for worst conditions.

❑ Trail status: Check closures, washouts, snow levels. NPS and WTA are good sources.

❑ Trip plan: Leave with emergency contact: route, timeline, vehicle, return time.

❑ Ten Essentials: Packed and verified. See our Ten Essentials field card.

❑ Phone + power bank: Both at 100%. Offline maps downloaded and tested.

❑ Vehicle: Full gas tank, spare tire, tow strap, jumper cables, blanket.

❑ Food + water: At least 1 extra meal and 1 extra liter beyond what you plan to use.

❑ Clothing: Layers for 20°F colder than forecast. No cotton — wool or synthetic only.

AT THE TRAILHEAD + ON THE TRAIL

❑ Sign in at trailhead register. Note where you parked.

❑ Confirm turnaround time with your group — commit to it.

❑ Stay on marked trails. Off-trail = #1 way people get lost.

❑ Check your map regularly, not just when you think you're lost.

❑ Drink water and eat snacks regularly. Don't wait until you feel it.

❑ Pace to the slowest member. Stay together as a group.

❑ If weather deteriorates, head back immediately. Don't push it.

COMMUNICATION + EMERGENCY

❑ Emergency contact filed: Name, phone, and your overdue protocol.

❑ Overdue plan: 'If I haven't checked in by ___, call 911 with my trip plan.'

❑ Cell phone: Try calling 911 even with no signal bars — connects via any carrier.

❑ Text 911: If call fails, try texting 911. Uses much less signal than a voice call.

❑ Satellite 911 texting: FREE for all carriers via T-Mobile/Starlink (emergency 911 only). Full messaging: $10/mo.

WHEN YOU RETURN

❑ Sign out at trailhead register. Notify your emergency contact you're safe.

❑ Report trail hazards to the land manager. Restock your emergency kit.

REMEMBER
The backcountry doesn't care about your schedule. The mountain will be there next weekend. If you filed a trip plan and you're overdue, we start looking along your planned
route. Be there when we arrive.
